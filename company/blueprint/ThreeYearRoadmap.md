# Three-Year Roadmap

## Objective

The first three years should prove that Sevora can repeatedly identify important problems, deliver exceptional systems, earn trust, and convert selected learning into owned products. The roadmap uses evidence gates rather than dates alone.

## Year 1: Establish the Standard

### Company

- Operate SevoraOS as the source of truth for decisions, research, delivery, and learning.
- Establish legal, financial, contracting, privacy, and security foundations.
- Define a disciplined weekly operating cadence and quarterly strategy review.
- Keep the team small and senior; add capacity only when a sustained constraint is understood.

### Market

- Conduct at least 40 structured operator and buyer interviews across no more than three priority industry areas.
- Select one initial industry beachhead based on problem severity, access, willingness to pay, and strategic fit.
- Launch the website with clear positioning, services, product status, and proof.
- Build ClinicFlow, RestaurantOS, or another evidence-backed industry demo; do not build both by default.

### Revenue

- Sell paid assessments and discovery before complex builds.
- Complete a small number of flagship engagements with direct founder or senior oversight.
- Establish baseline commercial metrics and avoid dependence on one client.
- Convert suitable deployments into defined ongoing partnerships.

### Products

- Audit Forge, FuelFlow, Ember, Astra, StockSage, and Raasta against a common scorecard: user, problem, evidence, distribution, strategic fit, and cost to validate.
- Select no more than two for active validation and one for primary investment.
- Use prototypes to retire weak assumptions before substantial engineering.

### Year 1 Exit Criteria

- three or more paid discovery engagements;
- at least two delivered systems with measurable customer outcomes;
- one credible industry demonstration tested with target users;
- one product hypothesis with repeated user evidence and willingness to pay;
- documented delivery, evaluation, and security standards; and
- sufficient cash discipline to choose work deliberately.

## Year 2: Build Repeatability

### Company

- Hire against proven constraints in product engineering, design, delivery, or commercial operations.
- Turn successful practices into reusable systems without creating premature bureaucracy.
- Establish formal quality reviews for product, AI behavior, security, and customer outcomes.

### Market and Revenue

- Deepen one or two industry positions rather than expanding broadly.
- Publish measured case studies and practical research through the [Proof Engine](ProofEngine.md).
- Build a referral and partner network around trusted industry operators.
- Increase recurring revenue through product evolution partnerships and the first paid product users.

### Products

- Move the leading product from validation to a narrow production release.
- Establish product analytics, customer support, reliability, and a repeatable acquisition path.
- Continue secondary product research only where it does not weaken the primary bet.
- Package reusable delivery capabilities without forcing every solution into a platform.

### Year 2 Exit Criteria

- a majority of new opportunities originate from proof, referrals, or repeat relationships;
- a defined solution pattern has succeeded with more than one customer;
- the primary product has retained paying users and evidence of repeatable value;
- recurring revenue covers a meaningful share of fixed operating cost; and
- no client or project can destabilize the company alone.

## Year 3: Create Compounding Growth

### Company

- Build clear ownership for products, solution partnerships, and company operations.
- Maintain one Sevora quality standard across client and owned products.
- Introduce leadership capacity only where it increases judgment and accountability.

### Market and Revenue

- Become the trusted premium AI Product Studio in the chosen areas of focus.
- Selectively pursue larger partnerships with stronger outcome evidence and pricing power.
- Make products and recurring partnerships a growing share of revenue.
- Expand to a new industry or geography only when the current model works without constant reinvention.

### Products

- Scale the primary product's reliability, distribution, and customer success system.
- Decide whether a second product has earned dedicated investment.
- Convert proven common capabilities into internal platforms only when they materially improve multiple products.

### Year 3 Exit Criteria

- repeatable acquisition exists for at least one owned product;
- products and recurring partnerships provide a credible path to becoming the majority of revenue;
- customer outcomes, referrals, and retention support premium positioning;
- the company can invest in products without endangering operating resilience; and
- Sevora's reputation is attached to a clear standard, not merely its founders.

## Quarterly Review

Every quarter, leadership should review:

1. Which customer outcomes improved?
2. Which evidence changed our market or product beliefs?
3. What should receive more investment, and what should stop?
4. Is client work strengthening or crowding out the product company?
5. Which risk to trust, quality, cash, or focus requires action now?

The roadmap is governed by [Business Model](BusinessModel.md), financed through [Revenue Model](RevenueModel.md), and made visible through [Website Strategy](WebsiteStrategy.md). Missing an evidence gate should change the plan, not lower the standard.
