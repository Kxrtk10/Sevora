# Non-Negotiables

Non-negotiables are boundaries Sevora will not cross for revenue, speed, growth, convenience, or competitive pressure. They apply to every employee, leader, product, customer relationship, and market.

## Integrity

We do not mislead, conceal material facts, manipulate evidence, or compromise our judgment for advantage. What we say publicly and privately must withstand scrutiny.

## Customer Trust

We do not exploit a customer's dependence, confusion, or lack of technical knowledge. We recommend what serves their interests, keep our commitments, and communicate risks before they become surprises.

## Product Quality

We do not knowingly release work that is unsafe, deceptive, or unfit for its intended use. Deadlines can change. Scope can change. The responsibility to deliver a coherent and reliable product does not.

## Honesty

We do not manufacture certainty. We distinguish facts from assumptions, capability from aspiration, and demonstration from production readiness. We admit mistakes and correct the record.

## Respect

We do not tolerate discrimination, harassment, humiliation, retaliation, or the abuse of authority. High standards and human dignity are fully compatible.

## Privacy

We collect only the information we can justify, use it only for understood purposes, and give people meaningful control where possible. Access to data is a responsibility, not an entitlement.

## Security

We treat security as a continuous obligation throughout design, development, deployment, and operation. We address vulnerabilities with urgency and communicate incidents responsibly.

## Long-Term Thinking

We do not exchange the company's reputation, independence of judgment, or future resilience for temporary growth. We consider who bears the consequences of our decisions and how those consequences compound.

## Craftsmanship

We do not normalize carelessness. We make tradeoffs consciously, review consequential work, and protect the details that determine whether a product deserves trust.

## Human-Centered Technology

We do not build technology whose business case depends on deception, preventable harm, or the removal of meaningful human agency. AI must have a clear purpose, appropriate oversight, and accountable ownership.

## Responsible Refusal

Some work should not be accepted.

We will decline customers, partnerships, features, data practices, or uses of our technology that conflict with these commitments. When commercial pressure and principle disagree, principle decides.

## Accountability

These standards have no exception for seniority, performance, or revenue contribution. Concerns may be raised without retaliation. Violations will be investigated fairly and addressed directly.

The credibility of this document depends on what Sevora is willing to give up to honor it.
