# Ember

## Purpose

A relationship and retention product for turning customer signals into timely, useful action.

## Current Status

Concept stage. The product direction should be refined through research, prototypes, and customer discovery before major investment.

## Future Vision

Ember should become a focused, high-quality product with a clear user, measurable value, and a path to recurring revenue.
