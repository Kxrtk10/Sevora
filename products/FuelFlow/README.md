# FuelFlow

## Purpose

A business operations product for tracking revenue, pipeline, delivery capacity, and execution health.

## Current Status

Concept stage. The product direction should be refined through research, prototypes, and customer discovery before major investment.

## Future Vision

FuelFlow should become a focused, high-quality product with a clear user, measurable value, and a path to recurring revenue.
