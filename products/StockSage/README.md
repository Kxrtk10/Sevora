# StockSage

## Purpose

An investing research assistant focused on disciplined analysis, watchlists, and market learning.

## Current Status

Concept stage. The product direction should be refined through research, prototypes, and customer discovery before major investment.

## Future Vision

StockSage should become a focused, high-quality product with a clear user, measurable value, and a path to recurring revenue.
