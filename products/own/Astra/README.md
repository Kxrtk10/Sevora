# Astra

## Purpose

A decision intelligence product for leaders who need clear visibility across strategy, metrics, and risk.

## Current Status

Concept stage. The product direction should be refined through research, prototypes, and customer discovery before major investment.

## Future Vision

Astra should become a focused, high-quality product with a clear user, measurable value, and a path to recurring revenue.
