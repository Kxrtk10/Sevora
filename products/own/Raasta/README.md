# Raasta

## Purpose

A navigation and planning product for local commerce, logistics, and route-aware service experiences.

## Current Status

Concept stage. The product direction should be refined through research, prototypes, and customer discovery before major investment.

## Future Vision

Raasta should become a focused, high-quality product with a clear user, measurable value, and a path to recurring revenue.
