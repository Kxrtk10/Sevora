# Forge

## Purpose

An internal and external toolkit for building reliable AI-enabled workflows, automations, and product prototypes.

## Current Status

Concept stage. The product direction should be refined through research, prototypes, and customer discovery before major investment.

## Future Vision

Forge should become a focused, high-quality product with a clear user, measurable value, and a path to recurring revenue.
