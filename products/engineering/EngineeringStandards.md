# Engineering Standards

Sevora engineering prioritizes working products, clean architecture, measurable quality, and maintainable delivery.

## Standards

- Build the smallest complete system that proves the product outcome.
- Prefer boring, reliable technology unless the product need justifies otherwise.
- Keep domain logic separated from UI, transport, and persistence details.
- Make important workflows observable through logs, tests, and clear state transitions.
- Document decisions that affect future engineering choices.
- Treat security, privacy, and data ownership as first-order product requirements.
