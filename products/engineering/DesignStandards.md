# Design Standards

Sevora products should feel calm, precise, and useful.

## Standards

- Design for repeated professional use before visual novelty.
- Make primary workflows obvious without tutorial text.
- Use clear hierarchy, restrained color, generous spacing, and accessible contrast.
- Prefer dense but readable operational screens for business tools.
- Avoid decorative UI that does not improve comprehension or action.
- Validate mobile and desktop layouts before calling a surface complete.
