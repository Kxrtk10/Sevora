# Services

## Service Architecture

Sevora offers a small set of senior-led engagements that move from clarity to working software to durable operation. Services are designed around decisions and outcomes, not labor categories.

Every engagement has a named owner, written scope, acceptance criteria, communication cadence, and agreed measures. Pricing follows [Pricing Philosophy](PricingPhilosophy.md).

## 1. AI Opportunity Assessment

**Purpose:** Determine where AI can create meaningful value and where it should not be used.

**Typical work:** Leadership interviews, workflow review, data and system inventory, opportunity scoring, risk assessment, and economic framing.

**Deliverables:** Prioritized opportunity portfolio, readiness findings, recommended first initiative, governance considerations, and a sequenced roadmap.

**Best for:** Leaders with strategic intent but insufficient clarity to commission a build.

## 2. Product Discovery Sprint

**Purpose:** Turn a consequential problem into a validated product direction.

**Typical work:** User research, process mapping, baseline measurement, solution design, technical assessment, prototype, and delivery plan.

**Deliverables:** Problem definition, user journey, prototype, architecture direction, success measures, scope, risks, and investment range.

**Best for:** A qualified problem that needs evidence before implementation.

## 3. AI Product Build

**Purpose:** Design, engineer, and deploy a production-quality AI-enabled product or workflow.

**Typical work:** Product strategy, experience design, application engineering, AI system design, integrations, evaluation, security, deployment, and adoption support.

**Deliverables:** The agreed working product, operational documentation, quality evidence, training, and outcome baseline.

**Best for:** Clients with an accountable sponsor, validated need, and implementation readiness.

Builds are milestone-based and intentionally scoped. A successful build may begin with discovery, but Sevora does not require implementation to follow advisory work.

## 4. Product Evolution Partnership

**Purpose:** Operate and improve a deployed system responsibly.

**Typical work:** Monitoring, maintenance, model and prompt evaluation, reliability work, security updates, analytics, user research, and prioritized product iterations.

**Deliverables:** Defined service levels, monthly operating review, improvement releases, and transparent performance reporting.

**Best for:** Products where continued learning, reliability, or AI behavior requires accountable stewardship.

## 5. Executive AI Advisory

**Purpose:** Support consequential leadership decisions without creating unnecessary delivery work.

**Typical work:** Portfolio review, architecture and vendor decisions, governance, investment review, product critique, and periodic strategic counsel.

**Deliverables:** Decision memos, recommendations, and scheduled leadership sessions.

**Best for:** Executives who need independent senior judgment over time.

## Engagement Progression

The normal path is:

**Assessment or Discovery -> Build -> Evolution Partnership**

This is not a mandatory funnel. Each stage must create standalone value, and the next stage begins only when evidence supports it.

## What We Do Not Sell

- commodity websites or undifferentiated application development;
- developer placement or staff augmentation;
- open-ended hourly capacity;
- AI theater, trend workshops, or prototypes presented as production systems;
- implementation without access to the problem owner and users; or
- responsibility without the authority and information required to deliver well.

Owned products such as Forge, FuelFlow, Ember, Astra, StockSage, and Raasta demonstrate that Sevora thinks beyond delivery toward product coherence, operation, and long-term value. Industry demos such as ClinicFlow and RestaurantOS demonstrate applied understanding before a client commits. Neither substitutes for honest evidence; their role in trust is defined in [Proof Engine](ProofEngine.md).
