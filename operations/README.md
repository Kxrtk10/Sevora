# Operations

Operations contains Sevora's commercial, financial, hiring, legal, and execution systems.

## Sections

- [BusinessDevelopment](BusinessDevelopment/) - sales process, discovery, ICP, services, and problem framing.
- [Finance](Finance/) - revenue model, pricing philosophy, and pricing ideas.
- [Hiring](Hiring/) - hiring roadmap and role planning.
- [Legal](Legal/) - legal templates, policies, and company governance notes.

Company-wide playbooks and decision records live in [company/playbook](../company/playbook/).
