# Problems We Solve

## Focus

Sevora solves consequential problems at the intersection of people, workflows, data, and decisions. We do not begin with a model or a feature list. We identify where capability is constrained, establish the cost of the constraint, and design the smallest complete system that changes the outcome.

## Core Problem Categories

### Time Lost to Repetitive Work

Skilled people repeatedly collect, re-enter, summarize, route, or reconcile information.

Sevora can design assisted workflows, document intelligence, structured automation, and human-reviewed agents. Success is measured in time returned, cycle-time reduction, error rate, and work redirected to higher-value activity.

### Fragmented Operational Visibility

Leaders cannot see the current state of revenue, delivery, inventory, capacity, risk, or customer activity without assembling information manually.

Sevora can unify relevant signals, create decision views, and surface exceptions that require attention. Success is measured in reporting latency, decision speed, forecast quality, and avoided surprises.

### Customer Demand and Retention Leakage

Leads, requests, follow-ups, and signs of dissatisfaction are missed or handled inconsistently.

Sevora can build intake, prioritization, response assistance, relationship intelligence, and retention workflows. Success is measured in response time, conversion, retention, resolution, and customer experience.

### Knowledge That Is Difficult to Use

Critical information is spread across documents, systems, and individual memory. Employees cannot reliably find the right answer in context.

Sevora can create permission-aware search, knowledge assistants, and guided workflows with traceable sources. Success is measured in search time, answer quality, onboarding time, and escalation reduction.

### Decisions Without Sufficient Context

Important choices depend on incomplete information, inconsistent analysis, or delayed signals.

Sevora can build decision-support systems that assemble evidence, explain uncertainty, and preserve human accountability. Success is measured in decision time, consistency, signal quality, and outcome improvement.

### Legacy Workflow Constraints

Existing tools force teams into duplicate work, brittle handoffs, and manual workarounds.

Sevora can integrate systems, redesign the workflow, or build a focused product where no suitable tool exists. Success is measured in handoffs removed, reliability, adoption, and total operating cost.

### Unclear AI Priorities

Leadership sees rapid technological change but lacks a grounded view of where to invest, what to avoid, and how to govern the work.

Sevora can assess opportunities, readiness, economics, and risk, then define a sequenced roadmap. Success is measured by decision clarity, avoided waste, validated opportunities, and execution readiness.

## Selection Test

A problem is suitable for Sevora when:

- it matters to a real person and accountable business owner;
- it occurs often enough or carries enough consequence to justify intervention;
- the current state and intended outcome can be observed;
- software can materially improve the result;
- required data can be used responsibly;
- the organization can adopt the new way of working; and
- the value supports premium product work.

## Problems We Avoid

We do not pursue technology for appearance, automation whose main purpose is indiscriminate headcount removal, systems without accountable human ownership, or custom builds where a sound existing product is the better answer.

## From Problem to Engagement

1. Quantify the present cost.
2. Observe users and the real workflow.
3. Identify constraints, risks, and baseline measures.
4. Test the highest-risk assumption with a prototype or proof.
5. Define the product and adoption plan.
6. Build, deploy, and measure.
7. Improve or stop based on evidence.
