# Website Strategy

## Role of the Website

The website is Sevora's public proof system. Its job is to help the right visitor understand what Sevora is, see evidence of how we think and build, and take an appropriate next step.

It is not a broad lead-generation funnel, an agency portfolio, or a collection of claims about AI. The site should feel like the product company Sevora intends to become: clear, calm, precise, and technically credible.

## Primary Audiences

1. **Qualified business leaders** evaluating an important AI or software initiative.
2. **Product users and design partners** evaluating Sevora's owned products.
3. **Exceptional future colleagues and partners** assessing the company's judgment and standard.

Each audience should find a direct path without forcing one homepage to explain everything.

## Core Narrative

The website should establish, in order:

1. Sevora is an AI Product Studio and technology company.
2. We build owned products and selected AI solutions for businesses.
3. We solve defined, consequential problems rather than sell development capacity.
4. Our work is grounded in craftsmanship, responsible AI, and measurable impact.
5. The visitor can inspect proof and choose a specific next step.

The primary company line is **Expand Human Potential.** Supporting copy should translate that purpose into concrete customer value rather than repeat philosophy.

## Information Architecture

### Home

Position Sevora, show the two building modes, feature the strongest proof, and route visitors to products, solutions, or a qualified conversation.

### Products

Give each of Forge, FuelFlow, Ember, Astra, StockSage, and Raasta an honest status, clear user, problem, and evidence. Concept-stage products should not be presented as established businesses.

### Solutions

Organize around business problems and selected industry understanding. ClinicFlow, RestaurantOS, and future demos should be presented as inspectable demonstrations with explicit maturity labels.

### Services

Explain assessment, discovery, product build, evolution partnership, and advisory in the language of decisions and outcomes. Reflect [Services](Services.md), not a menu of technical skills.

### Work and Proof

Publish case studies, demonstrations, product notes, evaluation methods, and selected technical decisions. This is the public expression of the [Proof Engine](ProofEngine.md).

### Company

Explain Sevora's purpose, principles, and long-term posture concisely. The website may summarize the Constitution but should not reproduce internal documents wholesale.

### Contact

Use a short qualification form covering the problem, its consequence, decision owner, current process, timing, and relevant constraints. Offer product waitlists or updates separately from client inquiries.

## Content Standard

Every published item should do at least one of the following:

- demonstrate understanding of an important problem;
- show a product decision and its reasoning;
- provide credible evidence of an outcome;
- explain a responsible AI or engineering practice; or
- help a qualified visitor make a better decision.

Avoid generic trend commentary, volume publishing, anonymous superlatives, and invented metrics. Fewer substantial artifacts will build more trust.

## Conversion Strategy

Primary calls to action should match visitor intent:

- **Discuss a business problem** for qualified solution buyers;
- **Explore the product** or **Join early access** for product users;
- **View the demonstration** for industry proof; and
- **Read the case study** for visitors still establishing trust.

The client form should qualify according to [Ideal Client Profile](IdealClientProfile.md). It should not promise a sales call to every submission.

## Measurement

Review monthly:

- qualified inquiries by source and problem;
- visits from target accounts and industries;
- proof-asset engagement;
- demo-to-discovery conversion;
- product waitlist quality and activation;
- assisted conversions from case studies; and
- language used by prospects to describe Sevora.

Traffic alone is not a success measure.

## Release Sequence

### Version 1: Clarity

Launch positioning, services, product portfolio, one proof artifact, company page, and qualified contact path.

### Version 2: Evidence

Add industry demonstrations, case studies, product progress, and deeper technical or product notes.

### Version 3: Distribution

Add product onboarding, targeted industry pages, partner pathways, and localization only where market evidence justifies them.

Implementation planning lives in the website directory. Strategic claims must remain aligned with [Competitive Advantage](CompetitiveAdvantage.md), commercial offers with [Pricing Philosophy](PricingPhilosophy.md), and company sequencing with [Three-Year Roadmap](ThreeYearRoadmap.md).
