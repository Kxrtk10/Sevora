# Mottos

Mottos compress Sevora's philosophy into language that can be remembered and used. They are not interchangeable slogans. Each has a defined context.

## External Taglines

### Primary

**Expand Human Potential.**

The clearest expression of why Sevora exists. Use it at the company level.

### Supporting

- **Technology with Purpose.**
- **Crafted for an Evolving World.**
- **Your Constant in an Evolving World.**

Supporting taglines may be used when the context calls for trust, adaptability, or craftsmanship. They should not compete with the primary tagline on the same surface.

## Internal Mottos

- **Raise the Standard.**
- **Build Better. Leave Better.**
- **Every Generation Should Build Further.**
- **Excellence Is a Responsibility.**
- **Think in Decades. Work in Days.**
- **Make the Useful Exceptional.**

## Product Principles

- **Start with the Human Need.**
- **Simple on Purpose.**
- **Intelligence That Earns Its Place.**
- **Return Time. Expand Capability.**
- **Make Trust Visible.**
- **Every Detail Serves the Whole.**

## Leadership Principles

- **Clarity Is a Form of Respect.**
- **Tell the Truth Early.**
- **Set the Standard You Expect.**
- **Create Capability, Not Dependence.**
- **Take Responsibility. Share Credit.**
- **Protect the Long Term.**

## Usage

Use these phrases sparingly. Their force comes from consistency between the words and the company's conduct. A motto should clarify a choice or reinforce a standard; it should never substitute for evidence.
