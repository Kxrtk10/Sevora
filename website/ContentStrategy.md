# Content Strategy

Website content should make Sevora feel credible, focused, and useful.

## Priorities

- Lead with product capability, not abstract AI enthusiasm.
- Use ClinicFlow as the first concrete proof point.
- Explain industries through problems, workflows, outcomes, and product references.
- Convert broad strategy into concise public language.
- Add case studies only when there is real proof to show.

## Content Rules

- Avoid inflated claims.
- Keep pages specific to buyer problems.
- Prefer screenshots, demos, product flows, and concrete outcomes.
- Link product and solution narratives without repeating them.
