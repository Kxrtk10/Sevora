# Animation Ideas

Animation should clarify product behavior, not decorate the site.

## Ideas

- ClinicFlow workflow animation from patient inquiry to confirmed appointment.
- Operational dashboard state changes when appointments, tasks, or follow-ups update.
- Solution page transitions that show fragmented work becoming a clear workflow.
- Subtle interaction feedback on product cards, navigation, and forms.

## Constraints

- Keep motion fast, restrained, and accessible.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not delay reading, navigation, or conversion.
